# Hi, I'm Teresa Ristow

I'm an I/O psychologist and people science researcher working across people analytics, measurement, and AI evaluation. I like figuring out what someone actually needs to know, choosing a way to study it, and making the findings useful for decisions.

## Selected work

### Conversation Drop-off Analysis

A response-length method for finding turns worth reviewing in research conversations. Includes synthetic controls, a reproducible example using public interview data, SQL checks, and an explanation of what the results can and cannot tell us.

[Interactive example](https://tristowww.github.io/conversation-dropoff-analysis/) · [Code and findings](https://github.com/tristowww/conversation-dropoff-analysis)

### Antiwork Subreddit Analysis

Exploratory analysis of management-related Reddit discussion over four years. Includes topic modeling, aggregate trends, robustness checks, and a separate whole-post sentiment analysis. The methods explain how much of the discussion the theme map covers and where interpretation needs care.

[Analysis and methods](https://github.com/tristowww/antiwork-subreddit-analysis)

### SIOP Conference Data Visualization

A look at AI-related session topics across five years of public SIOP conference programs. Combines data collection, cleaning, and an interactive way to explore the patterns and examples behind them.

[Interactive visualization](https://tristowww.github.io/siop-conference-data-viz/) · [Code and data workflow](https://github.com/tristowww/siop-conference-data-viz)
